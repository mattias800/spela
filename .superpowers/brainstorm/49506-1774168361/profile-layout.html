Developer Profile — What to show?

The curated overview should give a taste without overwhelming. Which sections earn their spot?

✓

Hero Banner

Company logo, name, game count, avg rating, platform count. Always shown — this is the identity.

?

About section

Company description (from IGDB), founded year, country, website/Wikipedia links. Collapsible — 3 lines with "Show more".

?

Top Games row (4-6 cards)

Horizontal carousel of their highest-rated games. Uses SpGameCard. "See all N games →" link navigates to full game list.

?

At a Glance stats

Compact stat chips: total games, active years, primary genre, platforms, avg rating. Currently a scrollable row of cards — could be simplified to inline chips.

?

Your Stats card

Personal connection: total play time with this developer, games played, favorites. Only shown if the user has actually played their games.

?

Release Timeline

Year-grouped release history with game thumbnails. Currently quite tall — could show only the latest 2-3 years with "See full timeline →".

?

Rating Distribution chart

Stacked bar showing Excellent/Good/Average/Poor. Compact visualization — takes little space.

?

Genre Breakdown

Genre chips with game counts. Could double as a filter that links to the games list filtered by genre.

?

Related Developers / Publishers

Chip links to other developers/publishers. Lightweight — usually just a few names.

Select all sections you want on the curated profile. Everything NOT selected gets moved to the full games subscreen, dropped entirely, or accessible via "see more" links. The hero is pre-selected (always shown).
