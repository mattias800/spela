Social & Competitive (your top pick)

1

Achievement Races

Challenge a friend: "First to unlock 'Defeat Bowser' in Super Mario World wins." Real-time progress tracking. Push notifications when your opponent unlocks one. Could tie into the existing Challenge framework.

2

Weekly Achievement Spotlight

Every week, one game is featured. Everyone competes to unlock the most achievements in that game during the week. Global leaderboard resets weekly. Creates a shared "what are we all playing" moment.

3

Rare Achievement Badges

Show what % of Spela users unlocked each achievement. Rare ones (< 5%) get a special gold border. Ultra-rare (< 1%) get a diamond effect. Your profile showcases your rarest unlocks. Bragging rights.

4

Social Achievement Feed

A live feed showing friends' achievement unlocks. "mattias800 just unlocked 'Speed Runner' in Sonic 2!" Reactions (emoji) on unlocks. Creates a sense of community and friendly competition.

5

Head-to-Head Comparison

Compare your achievement progress with any other user, game by game. Side-by-side view: "You: 15/30, Them: 22/30". See what they unlocked that you haven't. Motivation to catch up.

Visual & Celebratory

6

Beautiful Unlock Celebrations

When you unlock an achievement in-game: particle effects, sound, the badge flies in with a satisfying animation. 100% completion gets a special full-screen celebration. Make the moment feel EARNED.

7

Achievement Showcase on Profile

Pin your proudest achievements to your profile. "Featured Achievements" section — choose 3-5 to display prominently. Visitors see your curated best moments, not just a list.

8

Shareable Achievement Cards

Generate a beautiful image card for any achievement you unlock. Share on social media, Discord, etc. Shows the badge, game art, points, rarity, and your stats. Like Spotify Wrapped but for gaming.

Progression & Collection

9

Achievement Streaks

"7-day unlock streak!" — unlock at least one achievement every day. Shows on profile. Longest streak tracked. Motivates daily play. Break the streak and it resets (with a grace day?).

10

Meta-Achievements (Spela Badges)

Achievements FOR achievements. "SNES Master: 100% complete 5 SNES games." "Completionist: 100% any 10 games." "Speed Demon: Unlock 50 achievements in one week." Spela-exclusive, not from RA.

11

Achievement XP & Leveling

Every achievement gives XP (based on points + rarity). Level up your profile. "Level 42 Achievement Hunter." Visible on profile and leaderboards. Gives a sense of long-term progression.

Discovery & Motivation

12

"Almost There" Nudges

Home screen section: "You're 2 achievements away from 100% in Mega Man X!" Motivates finishing what you started. Shows the specific locked achievements with hints.

13

Difficulty Ratings

Each achievement gets a difficulty rating based on unlock % across all RA users. Easy/Medium/Hard/Insane. Filter achievements by difficulty. "Show me only the hard ones I haven't done."

14

"Games You'd Love" Based on Achievements

If you 100%'d Mega Man X, recommend Mega Man X2 and X3. "Players who loved these achievements also completed..." Smart recommendations based on achievement patterns.
