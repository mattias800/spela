The Arcade "platform" in Spela is a catch-all for many separate
arcade manufacturers (Capcom, Konami, Sega, Namco, Williams, etc.)
with no shared trademark. A generic text mark is the right framing
here rather than picking any one manufacturer's logo. The same
license terms cover the rasterised PNG variant in
../console-logos-png/arcade.png.
